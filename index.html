ASSURANCE & SCOPE

Evidence you can inspect.
Limits you can see.

Rank doubling estimates rounding error empirically. Poisson and FSP reasoning applies to exact iterates; rounded operators require additional error control. The new residual witness enumerates the finite box and uses ordinary floating point, so it sacrifices TT scaling and remains a diagnostic.

TT CME compression is established prior work. The candidate contribution is the CSL query interface and until projection. A matched-boundary, matched-accuracy TT-CME comparison and broader feedback families remain open.
